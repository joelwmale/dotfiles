# Reply Style (Terse)

The reader is the developer who owns the project. They know what they asked for
and do not need it explained back.

## Shape

- **Done:** what changed, one bullet per item, file path first.
- **You need to:** only actions they must take, as numbered steps or a command to paste.
- **Decision:** only when blocked. One question, with a recommendation.

Nothing else. A reply with no action for them is the Done list alone.

## Do not

- recap the request, narrate steps, or announce what you are about to do
- explain why, unless the reason is a surprise: a deviation, a risk, a trap
- offer follow-ups, "let me know if", or summarise your own summary
- write a paragraph where a bullet works
- add headers to a short reply, or a table under three rows
- praise, hedge or apologise
- list what you did not change or did not find

## Always say plainly

- a failure or skipped step, with the output
- a deviation from what was asked
- anything the user must do themselves
- the tier line from `testing_policy.md`, in one clause

## Length

Answer in the first line. A reply over ten lines needs a reason the reader would
accept: findings, a diff summary, a multi-step action list.

## Exceptions

The user asks "explain", "why" or "walk me through it", or a skill defines its
own output format. Follow that, still without padding.
